CASE STUDY · ENTERPRISE KERANDARI, JHARKHAND · 200 VEHICLES

Trip Card Intelligence for a 200-vehicle coal mine.

A mine does not need another dashboard. It needs to know which vehicle lost time, where the delay began, and what the next shift should do differently.

{{ s.v }}
{{ s.l }}
THE PROBLEM
  • Handwritten trip cards, maintenance logs and shift records pile up after every shift
  • The same vehicle is logged under a different ID in each record
  • Teams spend hours reconstructing events instead of improving the operation
WHAT WE BUILT
  • Trip records extracted from handwritten cards
  • Vehicle IDs reconciled into one history per vehicle
  • Maintenance and shift data linked, with utilisation shown and the original record one click away
HOW IT WORKS ON SITE From a handwritten trip card to the next shift’s decision.
